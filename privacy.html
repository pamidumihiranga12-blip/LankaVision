Privacy Policy for LankaVision Pro

Effective Date: September 10, 2026 | Developer: SMART ZONE LK

1. Introduction

Welcome to LankaVision Pro, Sri Lanka's leading job dispatch and technician management platform for CCTV, Satellite, and Router installations. We value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

To provide reliable services across Sri Lanka's 25 districts, we collect:

  • Account Details: Name, email address, phone number, district, and skill categories.
  • Job Listings: Service requests, descriptions, and approximate work locations.
  • Precise / Approximate Location (GPS): Used solely with your explicit consent when dropping a location pin on our interactive map or searching nearby jobs. Location data is never monitored in the background.

3. Customer Privacy Protection

Customer contact numbers are strictly shielded from public view (displayed as 07X XXX XXXX) and are only made accessible to an approved technician once a job has been formally claimed.

4. Third-Party Services

We integrate with secure cloud platforms:

  • Google Firebase: Secure authentication and real-time database hosting.
  • OpenStreetMap: Map tiles and geolocation display.
  • WhatsApp: Direct messaging for coordinated customer visits.

5. Data Deletion & Contact

Users may request complete deletion of their account and associated job data at any time by contacting our support team.
